Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026.

Hermes is a personal-use application used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs, Sheets) solely to perform tasks the owner requests.

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
